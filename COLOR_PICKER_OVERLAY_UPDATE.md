# Color Picker Overlay Update — 2.33.0

- Color information panel is draggable by its header.
- Minus button at the top-right collapses the panel into a small semi-transparent circle.
- Collapsed mode hides the movable pipette and sampling marker.
- Tapping the compact circle restores the panel and pipette.
- The color sampling logic remains unchanged: tap the pipette to sample the underlying screen.
