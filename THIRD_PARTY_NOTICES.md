# Third-party code and APKs

GITLS may use third-party libraries and open-source components. Their licenses and notices must be respected.

## Using another APK

Do **not** copy, decompile, extract, repackage, or embed another developer's proprietary APK/code merely to reproduce its features.

A feature can be implemented in GITLS when:
- the code/library is licensed for that use and its license requirements are followed;
- the service/API explicitly permits the intended use; or
- you implement the feature independently using public Android APIs.

An APK being publicly downloadable does not by itself grant permission to reuse its code, assets, branding, or proprietary functionality.

Keep license/attribution notices for any third-party components included in the project.
